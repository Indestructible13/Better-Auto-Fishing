# Better Auto Fishing
I've tried multiple other auto fishing mods, but none of them really had what I wanted. So I decided I could do better. 
This mod isn't super fancy, and it doesn't have every feature out there, but it works, and it has the features I need. Feel free to enjoy it with me or fork it and make it your own! :)

## Features
 - Hotkey to activate/deactivate the mod
 - Custom reel and cast delays
 - Randomizable reel and cast delays
 - Open water detection
 - Keep fishing while your inventory is open (can be disabled)
